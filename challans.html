Delivery Challans

A delivery challan moves goods without supplying them. Nothing entered here reaches B2B, B2CS, B2CL, the HSN summary or your tax liability — it appears only in GSTR-1 Table 13. The declared value is there because Rule 55 asks for it, not because tax is owed on it.
