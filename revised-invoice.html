Revised Invoices

Rule 53(1). Invoices raised between your effective date of registration and the day the certificate arrived were issued before you were registered — a revised invoice replaces each of them, within one month of the certificate. A revised invoice is not a new sale and not a credit note: it does not reduce a supply, it re-issues the tax document for one.
